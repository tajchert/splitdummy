1SplitDummy v2: icon-led pass. Same screens, fewer words
Assumptions
Warm paper tones, one terracotta accent, clean sans (DM Sans) with tabular figures for every amount.
Amounts always show the ISO code after the number (never a symbol alone); decimals follow the currency: 1,124.55 EUR · 184,500 JPY · 12.750 KWD.
Copy is English. Layouts leave about 30% extra room for longer languages, and number formatting follows the locale.
Demo group: Lisbon trip, 5 members, settled in EUR with multi-currency on. You are Maya, the owner.
Use the Tweaks panel to switch light/dark and try other accent colors.
v2: repeated controls and metadata (back, invite, undo, help, currency, members, round) become icons. Text stays where an icon would be ambiguous: amounts, statuses, warnings, primary actions.
Recognizable details
splitdummy
The logo is a coin split in two, with one half dropped a little. The same mark is the app icon and loading state.
A finish-line track runs Collecting → Settling → Settled and ends in a checkered flag. It appears on every group screen, so you always know how far you are from done.
